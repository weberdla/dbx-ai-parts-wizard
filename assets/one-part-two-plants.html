AI Parts Wizard

One part, two plants

East and West Tractor Works build different tractors, but they often buy the same physical part under different part numbers, from different suppliers, at different prices. The AI Parts Wizard finds those matches. A person approves each one and gives it a single enterprise part number, and both plants buy at the better price.
